FEMALE

Source: https://github.com/zer01dollars/female-body-atlas

MALE
Anatria3D uses real Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D male atlas geometry.
The male anatomy assets are CC BY-SA 4.0.
Source: https://github.com/Nurkan1/Anatria-3D
Pinned anatomy revision used by this build: 6320bd6

APPLICATION BEHAVIOR
The viewer fetches the real anatomy data at runtime using a primary URL and
fallback URL. No procedural anatomy, spheres, boxes, cylinders, or placeholder
organs are generated. The selected real mesh receives a visible pulse.
Selecting an organ does not auto-zoom the camera.
